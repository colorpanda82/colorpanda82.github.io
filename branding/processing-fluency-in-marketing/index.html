    Processing fluency in marketing

    Things that are easy to read, see and say are judged more true and more likeable. Reber, Schwarz and Winkielman set the effect out in 2004.

    Brave New teamAug 2026 · 3 min read

    FLU

    The ease with which the brain processes a stimulus is itself read as a signal. Clear names, legible type, repeated shapes and short claims feel more true than the same content made harder. The brain is grading the effort, not the content.

    It is the scientific case for consistency, and against cleverness that costs the reader effort.
