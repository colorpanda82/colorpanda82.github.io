Privacy and cookie policy

The short version: this site is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics, and collects nothing when you read it.

Who runs this site

Brave New Hive is published by Brave New, trading as Brave New Media. The registered entity is Sarron Creative Media SRL, fiscal code 38681961, registered in Bucharest, Sector 2, Romania. For anything on this page, write toanne@brave-new.com.

What this site collects

Nothing, when you read it. There is no analytics script, no tag manager, no advertising pixel and no embedded third-party player on any page. Nothing you do here is recorded by us.

Pages are served as static files. Our host keeps ordinary server logs, which include your IP address and the page requested, for delivery and security. We do not combine those with anything else or use them to build a profile.

Cookies

This site sets none, which is why you have not been asked to consent to any. It also stores nothing in your browser's local storage. If that changes, this page changes first and a consent request appears before anything is set.

The subscribe form

The form is not connected yet. Its button does nothing, and an address typed into it is not sent anywhere or stored. When the weekly email does start, this page will say who processes the addresses, where they are held, and how to get yours deleted, before the first issue goes out.

Links out

Links to brave-new.com and to sources cited in entries leave this site, and what those sites do is covered by their own policies. The share buttons on each entry are plain links too: nothing is sent to LinkedIn, X or your mail client until you click one, and nothing is sent to us at all.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected or deleted, and object to how it is used. Since this site collects nothing, in practice there is usually nothing to hand over, and we will tell you so plainly rather than send an empty form. Requests go toanne@brave-new.com and are answered within 30 days. You can also complain to your national data protection authority; in Romania that is the ANSPDCP.

Changes

The date at the top is the last time this page changed. It is updated whenever the site starts doing something the page does not already describe, not on a schedule.
