  1. HBG
    13 Sept 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    How Brands Grow. Byron Sharp (2010)

    The evidence-based case that brands grow by reaching more buyers, not by deepening loyalty. Built on decades of purchase data from the Ehrenberg-Bass Institute.

    Growth comes from being easy to think of and easy to buy, not from being loved by a few.

  2. OGL
    11 Sept 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    Ogilvy on Advertising. David Ogilvy (1983)

    The working manual of the man who built Ogilvy & Mather, on headlines, layouts, research and the difference between advertising that wins awards and advertising that sells.

    The consumer is not a moron. Write as if she were reading over your shoulder.

  3. POS
    9 Sept 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    Positioning: The Battle for Your Mind. Al Ries and Jack Trout (1981)

    The book that moved the battleground from the product to the prospect's mind. Own a word, or a place relative to the leader, before someone else does.

    You do not position a product. You position it in a mind that is already full.

  4. S1S2
    7 Sept 2026/Brave New team/4 min

    Thinking, Fast and Slow. Daniel Kahneman (2011)

    Kahneman's summary of forty years of research with Amos Tversky on how judgement works in practice: two systems, one fast and automatic, one slow and lazy.

    Most buying is decided before the argument is heard.

  5. 60/40
    5 Sept 2026/Brave New team/2 min

    The Long and the Short of It. Les Binet and Peter Field (2013)

    The IPA study of what produced measurable business effects across hundreds of campaigns: brand building and sales activation work on different timescales and need different creative.

    Activation pays this quarter. Brand pays every quarter after.

  6. ALC
    3 Sept 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    Alchemy. Rory Sutherland (2019)

    Sutherland's argument that the most valuable ideas in business do not survive a spreadsheet, because people do not choose the way economics assumes they do.

    The opposite of a good idea can also be a good idea.

  7. INF
    1 Sept 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    Influence. Robert Cialdini (1984)

    Cialdini's six principles of persuasion, reciprocity, commitment, social proof, authority, liking and scarcity, each traced to the research and the compliance professionals who use it.

    Every persuasion technique is a shortcut the brain already takes.
