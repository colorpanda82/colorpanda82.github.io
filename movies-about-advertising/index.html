    Explore Movies about advertising

    Films and series about advertising, persuasion, brands and the people who sell them. Year, director and what each one is really about.

    1. 2005
      5 Sept 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      Thank You for Smoking (2005)

      Jason Reitman's satire follows a tobacco lobbyist who can argue any side. A film about spin, and about the difference between persuasion and truth.

    2. 2016
      1 Sept 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      The Founder (2016)

      Ray Kroc turns a single hamburger stand into McDonald's. The product was the brothers'. The brand, the system and the name became his.

    3. DOC
      28 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      Art & Copy (2009)

      Doug Pray's documentary on the creative revolution in advertising, with Lee Clow, George Lois, Mary Wells Lawrence, Hal Riney and others on the work that defined the trade.

    4. 2011
      22 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      The Greatest Movie Ever Sold (2011)

      Morgan Spurlock makes a documentary about product placement, funded entirely by product placement. The pitch meetings are the film.

    5. BBC
      17 Aug 2026/Brave New team/3 min

      The Century of the Self (2002)

      Adam Curtis's four-part BBC series on Edward Bernays and how ideas from psychoanalysis were used to shape public relations, advertising and consumer culture.

    6. 1989
      11 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      How to Get Ahead in Advertising (1989)

      Bruce Robinson's comedy about an ad executive whose crisis of conscience grows into a talking boil that wants his job.

    7. 2009
      5 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      The Joneses (2009)

      A perfect family moves into a wealthy suburb. They are not a family. They are a stealth marketing unit, and everything they own is for sale.

    8. AMC
      30 Jul 2026/Brave New team/3 min

      Mad Men (2007 to 2015)

      Seven seasons of Madison Avenue in the 1960s. The Carousel pitch in the first season is still the best short explanation of what a brand sells.

    9. 1969
      24 Jul 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      Putney Swope (1969)

      Robert Downey Sr.'s satire: the token Black executive at a Madison Avenue agency is accidentally elected chairman and turns the place upside down.
