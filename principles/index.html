The handful of ideas most marketing still rests on, each with the person and the year it came from.

  • Strategy
  • Brand
  • Growth
  • Advertising
  1. 4P
    8 Sept 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    The 4 Ps of marketing

    Product, price, place, promotion. E. Jerome McCarthy set the frame out in Basic Marketing in 1960, and it is still the first thing most marketers learn.

  2. POS
    5 Sept 2026/Brave New team/4 min

    Positioning in marketing

    Al Ries and Jack Trout argued in 1972 that a brand is a position in the prospect's mind, not a list of features. The book followed in 1981.

  3. SEG
    1 Sept 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    Market segmentation

    Wendell Smith's 1956 paper in the Journal of Marketing separated product differentiation from market segmentation. Marketing has been splitting audiences ever since.

  4. USP
    27 Aug 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    Unique selling proposition (USP)

    Rosser Reeves, Reality in Advertising, 1961. Every ad must make a proposition the competition cannot or does not make, and it must be strong enough to move people.

  5. M/P
    22 Aug 2026/Brave New team/4 min

    Mental availability and physical availability

    Byron Sharp's How Brands Grow (2010) argued brands grow by being easy to think of and easy to buy, not by deepening loyalty among a few.

  6. 60/40
    18 Aug 2026/Brave New team/4 min

    The Long and the Short of It

    Les Binet and Peter Field, IPA, 2013. Brand building and sales activation work on different timescales, and the mix most often cited is roughly 60 to 40.

  7. AIDA
    12 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

    AIDA

    Attention, interest, desire, action. The sequence is usually attributed to the American advertising writer E. St. Elmo Lewis in the late 1890s.

  8. EQ
    6 Aug 2026/Brave New team/3 min

    Brand equity

    David Aaker's Managing Brand Equity (1991) and Kevin Lane Keller's customer-based brand equity (1993) gave the value of a name a structure you can measure.
