    A timeline of the moments that changed how advertising and marketing reach people, from the first agency to the first AI answer. Who invented advertising, when the first commercials ran, and what each change did.

    1. 1841
      7 Sept 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      1841. Who invented the advertising agency

      Volney B. Palmer opened what is generally regarded as the first advertising agency in the United States, in Philadelphia, selling newspaper space on commission.

    2. 1922
      3 Sept 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      1922. The first paid radio commercial

      On 28 August 1922, WEAF in New York aired a ten-minute talk paid for by the Queensboro Corporation to sell apartments in Jackson Heights.

    3. 1941
      29 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      1941. The first television commercial

      On 1 July 1941, a Bulova watch spot ran on WNBT New York before a Dodgers and Phillies game. It showed a clock face and a short line of copy.

    4. 1960
      24 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      1960. The 4 Ps of marketing are published

      E. Jerome McCarthy's Basic Marketing gave marketing its first widely taught framework.

    5. 1972
      19 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      1972. The Positioning Era Cometh

      Ries and Trout's series in Advertising Age moved the battleground from the product to the prospect's mind.

    6. 1994
      14 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      1994. The first banner ad

      On 27 October 1994, HotWired ran a banner for AT&T. Digital advertising began as a rectangle at the top of a page.

    7. 2000
      9 Aug 2026/Brave New team/2 min

      2000. Google launches AdWords

      In October 2000, Google opened self-service search advertising. The ad was matched to the search query. Intent became something a marketer could buy.

    8. 2022
      4 Aug 2026/Brave New team/3 min

      2022. ChatGPT is released

      OpenAI released ChatGPT on 30 November 2022. Within months, a growing share of buyers were asking a model for a recommendation instead of reading a results page.
